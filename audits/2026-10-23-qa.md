## Blocker

### QA-001 — No transactional email: guests get no booking confirmation and can't reset a password
- **Area:** Notifications / email infrastructure
- **URL:** https://alder.openship.org/booking/cmvlma2m103rclf01wnwxvqgh, https://alder.openship.org/account/reset
- **Steps:**
  1. Book and pay for a stay.
  2. Open the reservation page and look at the "Arrival & help" section.
  3. Go to `/account` and click "Forgot your password?".
  4. Query `{ guestAccountStatus { passwordResetAvailable } }`.
- **Expected:** A confirmation email goes out right after payment. Guests can reset their password. Contact-form inquiries reach the property.
- **Actual:** The reservation page still said "Confirmation email delivery: pending" more than 12 minutes after payment. `/account/reset` says "Password reset by email isn't available right now". `passwordResetAvailable` returns `false`. The contact form shows "Delivery status: pending". The lookup page says the confirmation number "appears in your booking confirmation email", but that email never arrives. A guest who books and closes the tab has no record of the booking.
- **Likely cause / file:** The SMTP environment is not configured in production. `features/keystone/lib/mail.ts:8` (`hotelMailInfrastructureConfigured` needs `SMTP_HOST/PORT/USER/PASSWORD/FROM`). Once SMTP is set, check that the outbox worker drains (`features/keystone/jobs/hotelOutboxJobs.ts`). This is an ops/config fix, not code.

### QA-003 — Rate calendar never shows nightly prices for most months, and stays over 14 nights fail with a generic error
- **Area:** Storefront / room detail / date picker
- **URL:** https://alder.openship.org/rooms/cmucnz5wl5h2xmo0e7mg6demz?checkIn=2026-12-01&checkOut=2026-12-21&adults=2

- **Likely file:** The `StorefrontRatePlan` GraphQL type doesn't expose `maximumStay` (or `advanceBookingMax`), so `features/storefront/lib/qa-workflows.ts:33` can never apply its check. See the type in `features/keystone/mutations/index.ts`, `lib/queries.ts` (`GET_ROOM_TYPE`), `features/storefront/screens/RoomDetailPage.tsx:119` (passes `maxNights: MAX_ONLINE_NIGHTS`), and `features/storefront/lib/use-rate-calendar.ts:52-60`. ## Business-sense gaps (day-one hotel needs)

These aren't bugs; they're things an independent hotel would expect to work on day one:
- **Only one room per reservation.** Families and small groups have to contact the property ("For several rooms, contact the property"). Most direct-booking engines allow 2–3 rooms in one checkout.
- **No visible promotions or packages** beyond a hidden promo-code field. There's also no "best rate guarantee" or other direct-booking incentive, which is the main reason guests book direct.
- **Policies are generic.** No pets, parking, smoking, age or ID, or extra-bed policy is published, and `/policies` tells guests to "ask ahead" about most of these.
- **No reviews or social proof**, and the gallery has only three photos in the lightbox.
- **Notifications** depend entirely on QA-001.

## What worked well
- No horizontal overflow, broken images or console errors on any page at 390px or 1440px. The design is polished and consistent.
- Pricing is clear. The full stay total with taxes and fees is shown before booking ($349.80 for 2 nights Classic Queen: $318 + $31.80 tax), and the same figure carries through to the Stripe payment and the statement.
- Validation is good: occupancy over the maximum, past dates, missing dates, and a disabled submit until required fields and the terms box are filled.
- Lookup with a wrong reference gives a neutral "couldn't match" message, and it works with lower-case references and mixed-case emails.
- Guest account claim works: a stay verified on the device is attached at sign-in. The .ics export is correct. The cancellation preview shows the refund, fee and deadline before confirming.
- Staff and guest separation holds: a guest account gets "Access Denied" on `/dashboard`. Anonymous GraphQL can't read bookings, guests or users.
