## Minor

### QA-006 — Not-found pages return HTTP 200, a regression from round 1 (R1 QA-011)
- **Area:** Storefront
- **URL:** https://alder.openship.org/nonexistent, https://alder.openship.org/rooms/nonexistent-room, https://alder.openship.org/rooms/cmvfwqsku0070o201cp68h910
- **Steps:** `curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}" <url>`
- **Expected:** 404, with the branded page.
- **Actual:** The branded "Find your way back to the house." page renders, but the status is **200** for all three. In round 1, `/nonexistent` returned 404. Next.js does add `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`, which limits the SEO damage. Monitoring, link checkers and crawlers still see the pages as successful. The not-found titles are also generic: "The Alder House · Direct reservations" for `/nonexistent`, and "Room · The Alder House" for a missing room.
- **Likely cause:** `app/(storefront)/loading.tsx` makes the route group stream, so the 200 status is sent before `notFound()` runs in `app/(storefront)/[...missing]/page.tsx` and `app/(storefront)/rooms/[slug]/page.tsx:44`. Move the not-found decision ahead of the streamed boundary (for example, drop the group-level `loading.tsx` for these routes or use `generateMetadata` plus a non-streamed check), and give `not-found.tsx` its own `metadata` title ("Page not found").
